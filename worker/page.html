How the checks work

Transparent rules inspect one retrieved HTML page. Registration dates come from public RDAP records; HTTPS confirms encrypted retrieval, not authenticity. A fee-language match needs human review. JavaScript-only content, CAPTCHAs, login pages, PDF pages, and unavailable providers may prevent checks. Contact addresses are not independently authenticated. This is rule-based OSINT, not machine learning.
